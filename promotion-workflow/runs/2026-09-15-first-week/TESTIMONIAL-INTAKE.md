# Capture an authentic experience

Draft interview/request template. Use only with a person whose experience with miABCTamil is established. A Karunya affiliation or pilot participation has not yet been confirmed.

## Request text

Hello [Name],

Would you be willing to describe your experience with miABCTamil in your own words? We would be interested in what you tried, what you found useful, and what could be improved.

We will share the exact proposed quote and attribution with you before using it publicly. Taking part is optional.

Thank you,
[Sender]

## Interview prompts

1. What did you use, and when?
2. What were you hoping to do?
3. What did you observe? Please give a specific example.
4. What was difficult or still needs improvement?
5. Is there anything you do not want quoted publicly?
