## Next experiment

Use responses from B01 to choose a topic. In the next cycle, test two English hooks for the same audience, visual, topic, and CTA:

- A: “Which Tamil word would you share first?”
- B: “What Tamil word do you use at home?”

**Hypothesis:** Asking about an existing habit may produce more relevant responses than asking about a future choice.

**Metric:** Relevant responses divided by reach, using equivalent seven-day reporting windows and the same platform definition. Define a relevant response as an answer to the learning question; count each account once per post where feasible.

**Decision:** Treat the result as directional. Reuse a hook provisionally only if it performs better across two comparable repetitions and the responses remain useful. With low reach or few responses, report insufficient evidence and inspect the answers for topic ideas. Organic distribution limits causal conclusions.
