miABCTamil

An introduction for school leaders

miABCTamil is a Tamil-learning app intended for children and adults. We would welcome a conversation with your school about its Tamil-learning priorities and whether the app could be relevant to your community.

Who the app is intended for

Children and adult learners are the intended audiences. An introductory discussion can help establish which learners your school wants to support and what they would need from a Tamil-learning app.

What we would like to understand

Which learners would you like to support, and what experience do they already have with Tamil?

Which language activities or conversations matter most to your teachers and families?

What product information would your team need before considering a closer look?

A focused first conversation

We propose a 15-minute introduction to hear your priorities, explain the project and agree on any information your team would find useful. Any further discussion can then focus on your actual needs.
