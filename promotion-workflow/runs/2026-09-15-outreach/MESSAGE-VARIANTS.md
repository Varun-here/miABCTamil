# Goal-based message variants

Draft v1. Bracketed content requires supplied facts. These are usable structures, not personalized or ready-to-send messages yet. Warm versions require an actual reply or conversation; if only an unanswered introduction exists, use EMAIL-DRAFTS.md's follow-up instead.

## Schools

### Cold short message

Hello [Name], I'm [Sender], [Role] at miABCTamil, a Tamil-learning app intended for children and adults. I'd like to understand [School]'s Tamil-learning priorities. Would you be open to a 15-minute introductory conversation?

### Warm follow-up

**Subject:** Next step on [confirmed topic]

Hello [Name],

Thank you for [specific conversation or reply] on [date]. You mentioned [accurate priority from their words].

Would it be useful to arrange a short conversation focused on that priority and whether miABCTamil could be relevant?

Best,
[Sender]

## Education journalists

### Cold short message

Hello [Name], I'm [Sender], [Role] at miABCTamil. [Specific, verified connection to their reporting.] Would background on our Tamil-learning app be relevant to your current coverage?

### Warm follow-up

**Subject:** Following up on your question about [topic]

Hello [Name],

Thank you for your question about [topic]. [Concise answer supported by the supplied source.]

Internal note: do not promise a study, launch, interview or attachment that is not available. If a substantive question cannot be answered from evidence, state what remains unknown.

Hello [Name], I'm [Sender] from miABCTamil, a Tamil-learning app for children and adults. May I send you a short discussion post for review, asking families which Tamil word they would like to share at home?

### Warm follow-up

**Subject:** Proposed Tamil-learning post for your review

Hello [Name],

Thank you for [actual response about the group's posting process]. Here is the proposed post:

Would you approve this wording for [Group]?

Best,
[Sender]

Internal note: the sender is affiliated with miABCTamil. Preserve that disclosure wherever the group's rules require it. Admin review is not proof that a post has been published.
